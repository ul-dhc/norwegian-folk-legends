## Photographic diffusion and reading pace

Childhood and study photographs now hold still for the first part of their scene, then slowly soften, expand by at most 4.5%, and become transparent. Liv uses the same treatment across her legend passages. All edges remain intact; no particles, generated pixels or cutouts are added. Pause holds the effect, Still map and reduced motion disable it, and View photograph retains the unchanged original. Legend timing now follows word count at 145 words per minute plus a short settling interval instead of the former 90-second minimum.

## Liv encounter: original-pixel background dissolve

The current encounter uses two registered copies of the original photograph. The foreground copy uses the existing soft SVG silhouette mask (`liv-original-mask.svg`); it preserves the original pixels of both people. As reading begins, the full background fades and the masked foreground emerges, with both layers sharing the same gentle 8% enlargement. Figures remain visible for subsequent passages. No generated cutout is used. Still map and reduced motion retain the intact photograph. This supersedes the earlier whole-photo disappearance for the Liv encounter only.

The whole page and all corners remain visible.
